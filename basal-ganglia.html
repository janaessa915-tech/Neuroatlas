Basal Ganglia

العقد القاعدية

The basal ganglia are a group of deep gray-matter nuclei located within the cerebral hemispheres. They include the caudate nucleus, putamen, globus pallidus, and functionally related structures. They play an important role in movement selection, motor learning and procedural behavior.

📍 Where are the Basal Ganglia?

The basal ganglia lie deep within each cerebral hemisphere, medial to the insular cortex and deep to the cerebral white matter. The caudate nucleus lies alongside the lateral ventricle, while the putamen and globus pallidus form much of the lentiform nucleus. Basal Ganglia · العقد القاعدية

The basal ganglia are interconnected nuclei located deep within the cerebrum. Their major components include the caudate nucleus, putamen and globus pallidus, with the substantia nigra and subthalamic nucleus forming important functionally related structures.

العقد القاعدية هي مجموعة من النوى المترابطة الموجودة في عمق المخ. وتشمل مكوناتها الرئيسية النواة المذنبة والبوتامن والكرة الشاحبة، مع اعتبار المادة السوداء والنواة تحت المهاد من البنى المرتبطة وظيفياً بها.

Caudate Nucleus · النواة المذنبة

The caudate follows the curvature of the lateral ventricle and has head, body and tail portions. It participates in motor control, learning and cognitive processes.

Putamen · البوتامن

The putamen is a large lateral nucleus positioned deep to the cerebral cortex. Together with the globus pallidus it forms the lentiform nucleus.

Globus Pallidus · الكرة الشاحبة

The globus pallidus is divided into internal and external portions and forms an important output and regulatory component of the basal ganglia circuitry.

Striatum · الجسم المخطط

The caudate and putamen together form the striatum, a major input structure of basal ganglia circuits.

Substantia Nigra · المادة السوداء

The substantia nigra lies in the midbrain and is strongly connected with the basal ganglia. Dopaminergic neurons here are particularly important for movement regulation.

Subthalamic Nucleus · النواة تحت المهاد

The subthalamic nucleus participates in the indirect pathway and helps regulate activity within basal ganglia motor circuits.

Motor Control · التحكم الحركي

Basal ganglia circuits help select desired movements and suppress competing motor programs rather than directly producing muscle contraction.

Learning · التعلم

The basal ganglia contribute to procedural learning, habit formation and reinforcement-based behavioral learning.

Internal Organization · التنظيم الداخلي

The nuclei are connected through parallel circuits involving the cortex, thalamus and brainstem, creating highly organized feedback loops.

Clinical Importance · الأهمية السريرية

Why the Basal Ganglia Matter

Disruption of basal ganglia circuits can affect movement, muscle tone, motor learning and behavioral control. Different components are associated with different neurological disorders.
