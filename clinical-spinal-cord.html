Spinal Cord
Localization

تحديد موضع الآفة في الحبل الشوكي

The spinal cord carries ascending sensory information and descending motor commands between the brain and the body. Because different tracts cross at different levels, the pattern of weakness, sensory loss, reflex changes, and the presence or absence of cranial nerve findings can help localize a lesion with remarkable precision.

الحبل الشوكي ينقل المعلومات الحسية الصاعدة والأوامر الحركية النازلة بين المخ والجسم. وبسبب اختلاف أماكن عبور المسارات العصبية، فإن نمط الضعف وفقدان الإحساس وتغير المنعكسات يمكن أن يساعدنا على تحديد مكان الآفة بدقة.

CASE 01 · CERVICAL CORD

The Weak Hands

ضعف اليدين مع علامات إصابة الحبل الشوكي العنقي

A 46-year-old patient develops progressive weakness in both hands, difficulty with fine movements, and later develops stiffness in the legs with an abnormal gait.

AGE 46 years
ONSET Progressive
KEY REGION Cervical cord
KEY CLUE Mixed UMN + LMN signs

History · التاريخ المرضي

The patient initially notices difficulty opening jars and buttoning clothes. Over several months, weakness develops in both hands. Later, the patient begins experiencing stiffness in both legs and difficulty walking. There is also reduced pain and temperature sensation across the upper trunk and arms.

لاحظ المريض في البداية صعوبة في فتح الأشياء وربط الأزرار. خلال عدة أشهر ظهر ضعف في كلتا اليدين. وبعد ذلك بدأ يشعر بتيبس في الساقين وصعوبة في المشي، مع وجود تغير في الإحساس بالألم والحرارة في الجزء العلوي من الجسم.

MONTH 1
Fine hand movements become difficult.
MONTH 3
Weakness of both hands becomes obvious.
MONTH 5
Leg stiffness and gait difficulty develop.
MONTH 6
Mixed motor and sensory signs are confirmed.
Neurological Examination · الفحص العصبي

What do we find?

01 · HANDS

Weakness of the Hands

There is marked weakness affecting fine movements of both hands.

Clue: lower motor neuron signs at the level of the lesion may involve anterior horn cells or nerve roots.
02 · LEGS

Leg Spasticity

Both legs are stiff with increased tone.

Clue: corticospinal tract involvement can produce upper motor neuron signs below the lesion.
03 · REFLEXES

Mixed Reflex Pattern

Upper-limb reflexes may be reduced while lower-limb reflexes are brisk.

Clue: mixed UMN and LMN signs can be highly localizing.
04 · SENSORY

Segmental Sensory Change

Pain and temperature sensation are altered over the upper trunk and upper limbs.

Clue: a sensory level supports spinal cord localization.

The combination of hand weakness with leg spasticity suggests a lesion affecting both segmental structures and descending long tracts.

A sensory change involving the upper trunk and arms suggests a cervical spinal cord level rather than an isolated peripheral nerve.

Upper-limb segmental abnormalities together with long-tract signs in both legs indicate involvement of the cervical spinal cord.

Cervical Strongly supported

The most coherent localization is a cervical spinal cord lesion affecting segmental upper-limb structures and descending motor pathways to the legs.

Region Cervical spinal cord

CASE 02 · THORACIC CORD

The Sensory Level

فقدان الإحساس أسفل مستوى محدد

A 38-year-old patient develops progressive leg weakness and difficulty walking. Examination reveals a clear sensory level across the trunk.

AGE 38 years
ONSET Progressive
KEY REGION Thoracic cord
KEY CLUE Defined sensory level

History · التاريخ المرضي

The patient notices that both legs feel heavy and increasingly difficult to control. Walking becomes slow and stiff. The patient also notices reduced pain and temperature sensation beginning at approximately the lower chest and extending downward.

لاحظ المريض أن ساقيه أصبحتا ثقيلتين مع صعوبة متزايدة في التحكم بهما. أصبح المشي بطيئًا ومتيبسًا، كما لاحظ انخفاض الإحساس بالألم والحرارة بدايةً من منطقة أسفل الصدر وامتدادًا إلى أسفل الجسم.

WEEK 1
Leg heaviness begins.
WEEK 3
Walking becomes stiff.
WEEK 5
Sensory changes become noticeable below the trunk.
WEEK 6
A clear sensory level is identified.
Neurological Examination · الفحص العصبي

What do we find?

01 · MOTOR

Bilateral Leg Weakness

Both lower limbs are weak with increased tone.

Clue: bilateral UMN signs suggest involvement of descending pathways.
02 · REFLEXES

Brisk Lower-Limb Reflexes

Deep tendon reflexes are increased in both legs.

Clue: this supports corticospinal tract involvement.
03 · SENSORY

Clear Truncal Sensory Level

Pain and temperature sensation change below a defined level on the trunk.

Clue: a sensory level is a major clue for spinal cord localization.
04 · CRANIAL NERVES

No Cranial Nerve Findings

Cranial nerve examination is normal.

Clue: this supports a lesion below the brainstem.

01 · Recognize the motor pattern

Bilateral leg weakness with increased tone and brisk reflexes indicates a long-tract motor lesion above the lumbar segments.

Tone Increased

02 · Find the sensory level

A defined sensory level across the trunk strongly suggests a spinal cord lesion rather than a peripheral neuropathy.

Normal cranial nerves make a brainstem lesion less likely and support a lesion in the spinal cord.

Because the sensory level begins around the trunk and the arms are relatively spared, a thoracic localization is strongly supported.

05 · Final localization

The bilateral leg UMN pattern, clear truncal sensory level, and normal cranial nerves localize the lesion to the thoracic spinal cord.

Region Thoracic spinal cord

Sensory Defined level

CASE 03 · HEMISECTION

The Half-Cord Pattern

نمط إصابة نصف الحبل الشوكي

A 29-year-old patient develops weakness on one side of the body below a spinal injury, loss of vibration and position sense on the same side, and altered pain and temperature sensation on the opposite side.

AGE 29 years
ONSET Acute
KEY PATTERN Crossed sensory findings
SYNDROME Hemisection pattern

History · التاريخ المرضي

After a localized spinal injury, the patient develops weakness of the right leg. Examination reveals increased tone and brisk reflexes on the right below the injury. Vibration and position sense are also reduced on the right, while pain and temperature sensation are reduced on the opposite side below the lesion.

بعد إصابة موضعية في العمود الفقري، ظهر لدى المريض ضعف في الساق اليمنى. أظهر الفحص زيادة في توتر العضلات وارتفاع المنعكسات في الجانب الأيمن أسفل مستوى الإصابة. كما انخفض الإحساس بالاهتزاز ووضعية المفصل في الجانب الأيمن، بينما انخفض الإحساس بالألم والحرارة في الجانب المقابل أسفل الآفة.

DAY 0
Localized spinal injury occurs.
HOUR 2
Right leg weakness becomes apparent.
HOUR 4
Right vibration and position sense are reduced.
HOUR 6
Contralateral pain and temperature loss is identified.
Neurological Examination · الفحص العصبي

What do we find?

01 · MOTOR

Ipsilateral UMN Weakness

Weakness and upper motor neuron signs occur below the lesion on the same side.

Clue: descending corticospinal fibers are affected.
02 · DORSAL COLUMN

Ipsilateral Vibration Loss

Vibration and position sense are reduced on the same side below the lesion.

Clue: dorsal column pathways ascend ipsilaterally in the spinal cord.
03 · SPINOTHALAMIC

Contralateral Pain/Temperature Loss

Pain and temperature sensation are reduced on the opposite side.

Clue: the spinothalamic pathway crosses within the spinal cord.
04 · PATTERN

Three-Pathway Combination

Motor, dorsal column, and spinothalamic findings create a highly specific pattern.

Clue: combining pathways is more powerful than interpreting one finding alone.

Interactive Localization Engine

This is a pathway-based localization problem. Identify where each major tract travels and where it crosses.

01 · Analyze the motor pathway

The corticospinal tract has already crossed in the brainstem before descending through the spinal cord. Therefore a spinal cord lesion produces ipsilateral motor weakness below the lesion.

Motor Corticospinal tract
Finding Ipsilateral weakness
Meaning Cord lesion

02 · Analyze the dorsal columns

The dorsal column pathway carries vibration, fine touch, and proprioception upward on the same side of the spinal cord until it crosses in the medulla.

Modality Vibration + position
Cord side Ipsilateral
Finding Right-sided loss

03 · Analyze pain and temperature

Pain and temperature fibers cross within the spinal cord through the anterior white commissure and then ascend in the opposite spinothalamic tract.

Modality Pain + temperature
Crossing Within spinal cord
Finding Contralateral loss

04 · Determine the side

Ipsilateral motor and dorsal column deficits identify the side of the hemicord involved. Here, those findings are on the right.

Motor Right
Dorsal column Right
Lesion side Right

05 · Final localization

The combination of ipsilateral corticospinal and dorsal column deficits with contralateral pain and temperature loss is the classic pattern of a spinal cord hemisection.

Region Spinal cord
Side Right hemicord
Pattern Hemisection syndrome
Case 03 Assessment · اختبار الحالة الثالثة

Master the pathways

CASE 03 · QUESTION 01

Which pathway carries vibration and proprioception?

Correct. The dorsal columns carry vibration, proprioception, and fine discriminative touch.
CASE 03 · QUESTION 02

Where does the spinothalamic pathway cross?

Correct. Pain and temperature fibers cross within the spinal cord.
CASE 03 · QUESTION 03

Why is motor weakness ipsilateral in a spinal cord hemisection?

Correct. Most corticospinal fibers cross in the caudal medulla before descending through the spinal cord.
CASE 03 · QUESTION 04

Which finding is contralateral in this classic pattern?

Correct. Spinothalamic pain and temperature loss is typically contralateral below the lesion.
CASE 03 · QUESTION 05

What is the best final localization?

Correct. Ipsilateral motor and dorsal column loss with contralateral pain and temperature loss is the classic hemicord pattern.

Clinical Localization Takeaway · الخلاصة

Spinal cord localization depends on recognizing patterns rather than memorizing isolated symptoms. Look for a sensory level, compare upper and lower motor neuron signs, determine which modalities are affected, and always ask where each major pathway crosses.

تحديد موضع الإصابة في الحبل الشوكي يعتمد على فهم النمط العصبي، وليس حفظ الأعراض بشكل منفصل. ابحث عن مستوى حسي واضح، وقارن بين علامات العصبون الحركي العلوي والسفلي، وحدد نوع الإحساس المتأثر، واسأل دائمًا: أين يعبر كل مسار عصبي؟
