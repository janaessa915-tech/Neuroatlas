Hippocampus

الحُصين

A curved structure within the medial temporal lobe that plays an important role in memory formation, spatial learning, and memory-related networks.

📍 Location

The hippocampus is located in the medial temporal lobe and forms part of the hippocampal formation.

⚙️ Main Functions

It contributes to the formation and organization of new memories, spatial learning, and contextual aspects of memory.

🔗 Important Connections

The hippocampal formation communicates with structures including the entorhinal cortex, fornix, mammillary bodies, and anterior thalamic nuclei.

🛣️ Related Pathways

Hippocampal information travels through interconnected memory circuits, including pathways involving the fornix and Papez circuit.

🩺 Clinical Relevance

Hippocampal dysfunction can affect memory formation and spatial learning and is studied in disorders involving memory impairment and temporal-lobe dysfunction.

🧠 Neurosurgical Context

The hippocampus is anatomically important in temporal-lobe surgery because of its proximity to structures involved in memory and other important neural networks.

NeuroAtlas Insight

Don't memorize the hippocampus as simply "the memory structure." Think of it as part of a larger network connecting cortical and subcortical regions involved in learning and memory.
