Brain Structure

Hippocampus

الحُصين

A curved structure within the medial temporal lobe that plays an important role in memory formation, spatial learning, and memory-related networks.

📍 Location

The hippocampus is located in the medial temporal lobe and forms part of the hippocampal formation.

⚙️ Main Functions

It contributes to the formation and organization of new memories, spatial learning, and contextual aspects of memory.

🔗 Important Connections

The hippocampal formation communicates with structures including the entorhinal cortex, fornix, mammillary bodies, and anterior thalamic nuclei as part of memory-related networks.

🛣️ Related Pathways

Hippocampal information travels through interconnected memory circuits, including pathways involving the fornix and Papez circuit.

🩺 Clinical Relevance

Hippocampal dysfunction can affect memory formation and spatial learning. It is studied in conditions involving memory impairment and temporal-lobe dysfunction.

🧠 Neurosurgical Context

The hippocampus is anatomically important in temporal-lobe surgery because it lies close to other structures involved in memory and language-related networks.

NeuroAtlas Insight

Don't memorize the hippocampus as simply "the memory structure." Think about it as part of a larger network connecting cortical and subcortical regions involved in learning and memory.
