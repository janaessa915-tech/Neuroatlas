Location · الموقع

📍 Where is the Hypothalamus?

The hypothalamus is located at the base of the brain, beneath the thalamus and forming part of the walls and floor of the third ventricle. It lies near the pituitary gland and is connected to it through the hypothalamic-pituitary system.

Hypothalamus · الوطاء

The hypothalamus is a collection of specialized nuclei that links the nervous system with endocrine and autonomic regulation. Despite its small size, it influences many fundamental processes required for maintaining internal balance.

الوطاء عبارة عن مجموعة من النوى المتخصصة التي تربط الجهاز العصبي بالتنظيم الهرموني والذاتي. وعلى الرغم من صغر حجمه، فإنه يؤثر في العديد من العمليات الأساسية اللازمة للحفاظ على توازن البيئة الداخلية للجسم.

Homeostasis · الاتزان الداخلي

The hypothalamus helps maintain stable internal conditions by monitoring and coordinating physiological processes.

Body Temperature · حرارة الجسم

Hypothalamic circuits participate in detecting and regulating changes in body temperature.

Hunger · الجوع

The hypothalamus contains circuits involved in regulating feeding behavior and energy balance.

Thirst · العطش

Clinical Importance · الأهمية السريرية

Why the Hypothalamus Matters

Because the hypothalamus coordinates many homeostatic, autonomic and endocrine processes, dysfunction involving hypothalamic circuits can affect multiple body systems.
