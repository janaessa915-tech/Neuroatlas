📍 Anatomical Location

The medulla is the lowest part of the brainstem. It lies inferior to the pons and continues downward until it becomes continuous with the spinal cord. In anatomical orientation, it is positioned near the base of the brain and sits in front of the cerebellum.
01 · Structure

Where is it?

The medulla forms the lower part of the brainstem, between the pons above and the spinal cord below.

02 · Function

What does it do?

It participates in regulation of breathing, cardiovascular activity, swallowing, coughing, and other involuntary responses.

03 · Connections

What connects to it?

Major neural pathways pass through the medulla, allowing communication between higher brain regions and the spinal cord.
