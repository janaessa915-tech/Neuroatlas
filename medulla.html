Medulla

The medulla oblongata is the most inferior part of the brainstem. It connects the pons with the spinal cord and contains important neural networks involved in breathing, cardiovascular regulation, swallowing, and protective reflexes.

02 · Anatomy

Important Structures

Pyramids

The anterior medulla contains the pyramids, which carry descending corticospinal motor fibers. Many of these fibers cross at the pyramidal decussation.

Inferior Olive

The inferior olivary complex participates in connections between the brainstem, spinal cord, and cerebellum.

Cranial Nerves IX–XII

The medullary region contains nuclei and pathways associated with important cranial nerve functions.

03 · Functions

What does the Medulla do?

Respiration

Medullary neural networks contribute to the generation and regulation of respiratory activity.

Cardiovascular Regulation

Autonomic networks in the medulla participate in regulation of cardiovascular activity.

Protective Reflexes

The medulla participates in important reflexes including swallowing and coughing.
