Medulla Oblongata

The medulla is the inferior portion of the brainstem and connects the pons with the spinal cord.

It plays major roles in regulating breathing, cardiovascular function, blood pressure and protective reflexes.

Medulla highlight

📍 Location

The medulla oblongata is the lowest part of the brainstem.

  • Superiorly connected to the pons
  • Inferiorly continuous with the spinal cord
  • Forms part of the floor of the fourth ventricle

🫀 Vital Functions

  • Respiratory regulation
  • Heart rate regulation
  • Blood pressure regulation
  • Swallowing
  • Coughing
  • Vomiting
  • Sneezing

🧬 Major Structures

  • Pyramids — contain corticospinal fibers.
  • Pyramidal decussation — major crossing of corticospinal fibers.
  • Olives — associated with the inferior olivary nuclei.
  • Important cranial nerve nuclei.

⚡ Clinical Importance

The medulla contains pathways and autonomic centers that control vital functions.

Because of this, serious injury affecting the medulla can have major consequences.
