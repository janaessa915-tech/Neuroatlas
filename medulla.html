⚡ Functions 🧠 Test Yourself

🔬 What is the Medulla?

The medulla oblongata is the lowest major region of the brainstem. It continues downward into the spinal cord and contains important neural pathways and nuclei.

🌬️ Breathing

The medulla contains neural networks that contribute to the automatic regulation of breathing.

❤️ Cardiovascular Control

Medullary centers participate in regulating cardiovascular functions such as heart rate and blood vessel activity.

🛡️ Protective Reflexes

The medulla participates in important protective reflexes including coughing, swallowing, sneezing, and vomiting.

🔗 Brainstem Connections

The medulla connects the pons and higher brainstem structures with pathways traveling to and from the spinal cord.

🩺 Clinical Relevance

Because the medulla contains pathways and centers involved in essential automatic functions, significant injury to this region can have serious effects on breathing and cardiovascular regulation.
