Medulla

The medulla oblongata is the lowest part of the brainstem. It connects the pons to the spinal cord and contains important centers involved in breathing, heart activity, and other essential autonomic functions.

📍 Where is the medulla?

The medulla is the lowest section of the brainstem. It lies below the pons and continues downward into the spinal cord through the opening at the base of the skull.

About the Medulla

The medulla oblongata is an important part of the brainstem. It contains neural pathways connecting the brain and spinal cord and contributes to the regulation of vital involuntary functions.
