Medulla

The medulla oblongata is the most inferior part of the brainstem. It connects the pons with the spinal cord and participates in breathing, cardiovascular regulation, swallowing, and protective reflexes.

Medulla Anatomy

Important Structures

Pyramids

The pyramids contain descending corticospinal motor fibers. Many of these fibers cross at the pyramidal decussation.

Inferior Olive

The inferior olivary complex participates in important connections between the brainstem and cerebellum.

Cranial Nerves IX–XII

The medullary region contains important cranial nerve nuclei and pathways associated with swallowing, tongue movement, and visceral functions.
