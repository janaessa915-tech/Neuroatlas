Medulla

The medulla oblongata is the most inferior part of the brainstem. It connects the pons with the spinal cord and contains important pathways and neural networks involved in breathing, cardiovascular regulation, swallowing, and protective reflexes.

Important Structures

Pyramids

The anterior medulla contains the pyramids, which carry descending corticospinal motor fibers. Many cross at the pyramidal decussation.

Inferior Olive

The inferior olivary complex participates in connections involving the brainstem, spinal cord, and cerebellum.

Cranial Nerves IX–XII

The medullary region contains nuclei and pathways associated with important cranial nerve functions.
