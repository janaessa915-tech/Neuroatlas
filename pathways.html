Follow the Journey

Every individual pathway page in NeuroAtlas is designed around the same sequence so you can compare pathways efficiently.

STEP 01

Origin

Identify where the pathway begins and which neurons or receptors provide its initial input.

STEP 02

Course

Follow the anatomical route through the brain, brainstem, spinal cord, or peripheral nervous system.

STEP 03

Crossing

Learn where fibers decussate and how crossing patterns affect neurological localization.

STEP 04

Destination

Identify the final nucleus, cortical region, or target structure and connect it to function.
