Study Strategy · طريقة الدراسة

Learn Every Pathway the Same Way

01 · ORIGIN

Where does it start?

Identify the receptor, cortical region, nucleus, or other structure where the pathway begins.

02 · COURSE

Where does it travel?

Follow the pathway through the brainstem, spinal cord, white matter, or other anatomical regions.

03 · CROSSING

Where does it cross?

Learn whether and where fibers decussate. This is critical for understanding neurological localization.

04 · DESTINATION

Where does it end?

Identify the final nucleus, cortical region, cerebellar region, spinal segment, or other destination.

05 · CLINICAL

What happens if it is damaged?

Connect the pathway to the expected neurological deficit and lesion localization.

06 · CONNECT

What structures surround it?

Relate the pathway to nearby nuclei, tracts, vessels, ventricles, and other clinically important structures.
