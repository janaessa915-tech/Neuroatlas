Study Method

How to Study Neural Pathways

01

Find the Origin

Identify where the pathway starts and which neurons or receptors provide its initial input.

02

Follow the Course

Trace the pathway through the important anatomical structures, relays, and crossings.

03

Know the Destination

Finish by identifying the final target and connecting pathway anatomy with its function.
