Brainstem

Pons

الجسر

The pons is the middle portion of the brainstem. It connects important regions of the brain and spinal cord and contributes to movement, sensation, facial functions, and regulation of breathing and sleep.

🔗 Neural Connections

The pons contains pathways connecting the cerebral cortex, cerebellum, and spinal cord.

🚶 Movement

Pontine pathways help communicate with the cerebellum and contribute to the coordination of movement.

👁️ Cranial Nerves

Several cranial nerve nuclei are associated with the pons, including structures involved in facial sensation, facial movement, hearing, and balance.

🌬️ Breathing & Sleep

Pontine networks participate in regulating breathing patterns and transitions between different sleep states.

🩺 Clinical Relevance

Damage to the pons can affect movement, sensation, facial function, coordination, breathing, and consciousness.

📍 Where is the Pons?

The pons lies between the midbrain and medulla and forms the middle portion of the brainstem.
